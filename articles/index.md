# Articles

### Vignettes

- [Using metafor and data frames as
  input](https://joonho112.github.io/bayesEfron/articles/data-input.md):

  Getting estimates and standard errors into the model from a data
  frame, a list or metafor, and labeling the results.

- [Choosing the grid and comparing
  models](https://joonho112.github.io/bayesEfron/articles/choosing-a-grid.md):

  What the grid and the spline basis do, and how to check whether the
  results depend on them.

- [Checking convergence and
  plotting](https://joonho112.github.io/bayesEfron/articles/diagnostics.md):

  Reading the convergence diagnostics of a fit, acting on the warnings,
  and the four plots.

- [The model](https://joonho112.github.io/bayesEfron/articles/model.md):

  The deconvolution problem, the log-spline family for the distribution
  of effects, its prior, and the posterior distribution of a site
  effect.

- [Implementation and
  checks](https://joonho112.github.io/bayesEfron/articles/implementation.md):

  Reproducing a fit, the Stan model, the checks the package makes,
  coverage on a benchmark with known effects, and the notation of the
  paper.
